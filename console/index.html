    Chakra Console Guide

    The Chakra Console is the web dashboard where your organization manages licenses, monitors usage, tunes talk-time limits, and customizes voice clones without code changes.

    First Sign-in

    1. Open the Chakra Console address in your browser.
    2. Sign in with the organization email and initial password provided by Chakra Labs.
    3. Navigate to Account and update your password (at least 10 characters).

    Console Views & Features

    1. Dashboard

    The central overview of your organization's voice activity: - Usage Metrics: Total calls, aggregate minutes consumed, and average call duration for the current billing cycle. - Package Allowances: View your active monthly minute allowance, concurrency limits, and copy your CHAKRA_LICENSE_KEY. - Pace & Projections: Forecasts expected month-end minute consumption based on recent calling volume. - Activity Visualizations: Interactive heatmaps of peak calling hours and daily usage charts.

    2. Project View

    Each project represents a specific deployment (e.g., telephone helpline, web assistant): - View project type (phone, web_voice, web_chat). - Check active LiveKit room dispatches and per-agent pipeline allocations. - Monitor channel-specific consumption (voice minutes vs. chat token counts).

    3. Talk-Time Limits

    Enforce daily allowances to prevent abuse and manage operational costs: - Per-Caller Tracking: Cumulative call duration is tracked against each caller's unique telephone number. - Midnight Resets: Quotas reset every night at midnight (Sri Lanka Time / Asia/Colombo). - Graceful Warning: Nearing callers hear a polite warning prompt before the session concludes. - Dynamic Propagation: Changes made in the Console take effect on running workers within 10 minutes without service restarts.

    4. Voice Customization & Cloning

    Personalize the voice persona for your brand: - Preset Voices: Select from Chakra Labs' curated library of expressive voices. - Voice Cloning (Chakra Voice): 1. Upload a 5 to 10-second .wav audio clip of the target speaker recorded in a quiet environment. 2. Enter the exact transcript of the words spoken (e.g. in Sinhala). The transcript must match the spoken audio precisely. 3. The cloned voice propagates to your live agents within minutes. Existing calls preserve their starting voice to prevent mid-call disruption.

    Important Timings & Billing

    • Monthly Reset: Usage quotas reset on the 1st of every calendar month (Sri Lanka time).
    • Dashboard Refresh: Telemetry updates in the Console approximately every 2 minutes after a call completes.
    • Configuration Propagation: Voice changes and talk-time quota modifications synchronize to agents within 10 minutes.
